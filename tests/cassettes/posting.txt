Technical Product Manager, Life Sciences Data Platform

Northstar Bioinformatics builds data products used by research, clinical, and
commercial teams across the life-sciences industry. We are hiring a Technical
Product Manager to own the platform that turns partner and reference datasets
into standardized, trusted, and reusable data products.

Responsibilities

- Own the roadmap and backlog for ingestion, schema reconciliation, reference
  data, entity resolution, lineage, and data-quality capabilities.
- Run discovery with scientists, data stewards, customers, and engineers, then
  turn their needs into requirements, specifications, user stories, acceptance
  criteria, release plans, and product demonstrations.
- Define API contracts, mappings, transformations, validation rules, and
  measurable outcomes for new data connectors.
- Guide engineering and quality-assurance teams through delivery while making
  clear prioritization decisions across competing datasets and stakeholders.
- Improve how canonical records are matched, deduplicated, governed, and
  synchronized with external systems.
- Document data definitions, provenance, and permitted uses so downstream
  analytics and AI products can use the data safely.

Required qualifications

- Three or more years of software product-management experience.
- Experience managing a data platform or technical data product through the
  full development lifecycle.
- Strong SQL skills and hands-on familiarity with ETL or ELT pipelines, APIs,
  data mapping, schema design, and data validation.
- Demonstrated backlog management, requirements writing, customer discovery,
  and cross-functional delivery in an Agile environment.
- Clear communication with scientific, business, and engineering audiences.

Preferred qualifications

- Experience in life sciences, healthcare, clinical research, or drug data.
- Direct experience with an enterprise master data management platform.
- Familiarity with data catalogs, terminology systems, and data governance.
- Background in software or data engineering.

Remote within Canada. Applicants must be authorized to work in Canada without
sponsorship. The base salary range is CAD 125,000 to CAD 155,000.
